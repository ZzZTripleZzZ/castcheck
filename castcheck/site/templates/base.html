Raw model output, no post-processing. Values are interpolated straight from each model's native 0.25° grid with no MOS, bias correction, downscaling or other post-processing, and are sampled only at 00/06/12/18 UTC. The headline scores the forecast at those instants against the observation at the same instants; the comparison against the true NWS daily extremes is published separately and carries a sampling penalty (methodology §2.3).
