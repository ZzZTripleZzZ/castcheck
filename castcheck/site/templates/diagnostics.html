What this page does and does not claim

The three figures below describe a pattern in these measurements. They are not yet attributed to any cause, because the analyses that would separate the candidates have not been run. Three explanations are all consistent with what is drawn here, and this page excludes none of them:

C. The sampling penalty, model by model

The like-for-like extreme tmax_s scores a model's four forecast samples against the highest of the four observations at the same instants; tmax_cli scores exactly the same four forecast samples against the true NWS daily maximum. The difference is the sampling penalty, in °F, at lead day {{ lead }}.

These numbers move

Every figure and table on this page is rebuilt from scratch on each publication, over a rolling {{ window }} window, so the sample behind it grows every day and the ordering of models can change. Nothing here is a fixed result: the record is short, the confidence intervals published on the leaderboard are wide, and a difference visible today may not survive another month of data. The JSON carries the same numbers with their sample sizes, and each row links to the model page where the intervals live. Next rebuild: {{ next_update_human }}.
