The 6-hourly instantaneous layer

Everything on this site is derived from two tables that are published in full, because a daily extreme is a definition and someone checking a claim should not have to accept ours:

  • forecast_values — one row per model run, valid time, station, variable and interpolation method: the raw 2 m temperature at 00/06/12/18 UTC, before any extreme is taken. Parquet shards at {{ hf_files_url }} under data/forecast_values/model_id=<model>/year_month=YYYY-MM.parquet; the signed daily errors derived from them are in the daily_errors/<station>.csv.gz files above — one row per model, initialization, lead day, method, variable and day, and four rows a day for the pooled t2, with valid_hour_utc naming the instant.
  • truth_instant — the observed 2 m temperature at those same four instants: the routine METAR nearest the synoptic hour within ±35 minutes, from the IEM ASOS archive (data/truth_instant/year=YYYY.parquet in the same dataset). This is the truth for the headline t2 variables and for tmax_s/tmin_s; the NWS Daily Climate Report in truth_daily is the truth for the secondary *_cli variables.
