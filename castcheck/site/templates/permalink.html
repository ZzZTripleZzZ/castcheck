All four windows × both initializations × both interpolations × every variable. Rows are grouped by variable and the specification is collapsed into one monospace key (window · init · method) so the eye travels straight to the numbers. The row matching the default view is highlighted. Errors are computed in °C and shown in °F; CI is a 95 % moving-block bootstrap interval on this group's own days, and reads — when it could not be computed (fewer than {{ ci_min_n }} scored days or fewer than {{ ci_min_blocks }} blocks). The ±1 °F interval is a Wilson score interval, not a bootstrap.

Skill divides by the persistence MAE on the days both have a value; that denominator and the size of the intersection are printed under the number, so the score can be checked rather than taken on trust. MAE debiased and skill, debiased remove a bias estimated on the 30 scored days before each day and applied forward — out of sample, never fitted on the day being scored; n debiased is how many days had enough history to qualify. n QC next to n counts days whose observation carries a QC flag.
