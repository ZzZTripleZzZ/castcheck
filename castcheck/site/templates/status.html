Model runslast {{ report.days }} days

A model run counts as complete only when every station has a value at every expected forecast step (the analysis step f000 is not one); nothing is silently skipped (methodology §6). Truth is the first final NWS Daily Climate Report, which can only exist from the day after the climatological day.

Completeness is counted from each model's own period start — the first initialization CastCheck ever held for it — and only over initializations the upstream archive actually produced. The NOAA/CIRA 0.25° AIWP bucket publishes the GFS-initialized models on alternating cycles, so about half of their nominal 00Z/12Z slots were never produced upstream; those are drawn as empty slots, marked not produced upstream, and are not counted as downtime. Days before a model entered the record are treated the same way. Oldest day on the left.

Truth (first-final NWS CLI)last {{ report.days }} days

The first final Daily Climate Report is issued after the station's local midnight, one to four hours after it, so the deadline is per station: at 06 UTC yesterday's report exists for New York and cannot exist yet for Los Angeles. Days whose deadline has not passed are drawn grey and are neither a gap nor part of the percentage.

Observed instantslast {{ report.days }} days

The truth of the headline t2 score is the routine METAR nearest each of 00/06/12/18 UTC, within ±35 minutes (truth_instant). A day counts as complete only with all four: a station that quietly loses one instant a day loses a quarter of its headline sample without anything else turning red. Today is never counted — its 18Z instant has not happened — and neither are the days before a station's own archive begins.
